Patel Chashma Ghar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · App: Patel Chashma Ghar · Owner: Patel Chashma Ghar, Chanakyapuri

1. About

Patel Chashma Ghar is an app for our optical store. It shows our products (frames, sunglasses, lenses, contact lenses) and lets customers contact us to order.

2. What we collect

The app itself does not create accounts and does not collect personal data on any server. Your cart, favourites and settings are stored only on your device.

3. WhatsApp orders

When you tap "WhatsApp par order karo", your phone opens WhatsApp with a ready message containing the items you selected. The message is sent by you, through WhatsApp, to our store number. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.

4. Photos and camera

Photos are used only inside the app (for product pictures you add as the store owner) and stay on your device. We do not upload them anywhere.

5. Payments

Payment is made directly to the store by UPI or cash. The app does not process or store any card or bank details.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change.
